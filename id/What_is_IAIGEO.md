# IAIGEO

## What is IAIGEO?

IAIGEO 是由 Kevin Liu 创建的独立研究与技术实践平台，专注于 AI Search、GEO/AEO、RAG 信息检索、企业实体与语义一致性，以及 AI-Ready Business / AI-Ready Website。

IAIGEO 研究企业信息如何被 AI 系统发现、理解、检索、引用和准确表示，并将研究成果应用于企业数字信息架构与 AI-ready digital presence。

## English Description

IAIGEO is an independent research and applied technology platform founded by Kevin Liu, focused on AI Search, GEO/AEO, RAG retrieval, enterprise entity and semantic alignment, and AI-Ready Business / AI-Ready Website.

IAIGEO studies how enterprise information can be discovered, understood, retrieved, cited, and accurately represented by AI systems, and applies this research to enterprise digital information architecture and AI-ready digital presence.
